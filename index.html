About

I'm studying Communication and Multimedia Design at the Rotterdam University of Applied Sciences. Since childhood I've been fascinated by how things work — taking apart toys, then more complex devices — and that curiosity grew into a passion for design and technology. I love capturing images and creating designs to tell stories, whether that's product photography, portrait photography, or UX/service design work.

I've worked on projects with IceMobile, PostNL, Stichting DOCK, Picnic, Clubleaf, and Wereldmuseum Amsterdam.
